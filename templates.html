Academic Formal

Scholarly, respectful correspondence for academic institutions

SUBJECT
Research Assistant Position Inquiry - Fall 2024
FROM
Emily Chen
TO
Dr. Robert Williams
EMAIL BODY
Dear Professor Williams, I hope this email finds you well. My name is Emily Chen, and I am a third-year undergraduate student majoring in Computer Science at University College. I am writing to express my strong interest in joining your research team as a Research Assistant for the Fall 2024 semester. I have been following your work on machine learning applications in healthcare, particularly your recent publication in the Journal of AI Research, which I found profoundly inspiring. My coursework in Advanced Algorithms and Data Structures, combined with my experience in Python and TensorFlow, has prepared me to contribute meaningfully to your ongoing projects. I have attached my resume and transcript for your review. I would be grateful for the opportunity to discuss how I might contribute to your research endeavors. Would you be available for a brief meeting to explore this possibility? Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to hearing from you. Best regards, Emily Chen Computer Science Major University College emily.chen@university.edu
